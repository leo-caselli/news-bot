The study, published in the British Medical Journal , external , also found dementia was common among brain donors. Daneshvar, an associate professor at Harvard Medical School. "These findings indicate that NFL players have a higher prevalence of CTE at death than has been previously shown in multiple community based studies." Researchers calculated a "conservative minimum" of CTE prevalence among those who died in the six-year period to be 24.5% - rising to a "maximum" 97.7%.
